WORKPLACE AMBITION

01

Warm-up

Give a full answer + one detail. Your teacher asks “Why?” 5 min
ROUTINE What errands do you usually run before a busy work week?
CHANGE Have you ever got promoted or changed jobs? What happened?
EXPERIENCE Have you ever worked in a demanding or unpredictable environment?
AMBITION Are you reaching for the stars right now — or playing it safe?
02

Career narrative — past & present in context

Notice → choose → explain → personalise 20 min
A • First day at the magazine — complete the story

Use Past Simple, Present Perfect, or Present Perfect Continuous. Then justify each choice.

Andrea (have) an appointment with Human Resources at nine, but she (arrive) late because her train (break down). She never (work) in fashion before, and she (feel) nervous since she entered the building. Miranda (be) a huge deal in the industry for years. The editor in chief already (reject) three layouts this morning. Emily (man) the desk all day and (deal) with errands since lunch. Andrea (chain) to that desk for six hours, but she not (quit) yet.

Meaning check: Which events are finished? Which connect past to now? Which emphasise duration?
Personal link: Change two details so the story becomes true for you or someone you know.
Retell: Close the text and retell it from memory using “I” instead of “she”.
B • Error clinic — diagnose and repair

Each sentence has one tense or form error. Correct it and explain how the meaning changes.

  1. I have an appointment yesterday at nine.
  2. She works here since March.
  3. Did you ever got promoted?
  4. Emily is manning the desk all day yesterday.
  5. Miranda is a huge deal for twenty years.
  6. I am bursting since this morning.
  7. He didn't sack yet.
  8. We deal with the schedule expenses now for two weeks.
  9. She pretends she didn't just ask me that.
  10. My head is on the chopping block last month.
Teacher challenge: Your teacher changes one time marker. Correct the tense immediately.
C • Career-switch interview

Answer aloud. Then switch perspective without changing the topic. Ask your teacher one follow-up question.

1 • past job → current role

What did you do in your last job?

What have you been doing differently since you started?

Follow up: How long have you been in this position?
2 • ambition → reality

What promotion would you love to be considered for?

What has stopped you so far?

Follow up: Is an interest in your field crucial for success?
3 • routine → crisis

What errands do you usually handle at work?

What went wrong the last time everything was not going according to plan?

Follow up: Who had to deal with it?
4 • colleague → self

Has a colleague ever been blamed for something unfairly?

Have you ever had to stand up for yourself at work?

Follow up: What was the result?
5 • pressure → balance

When did you last feel under pressure?

What have you been doing to improve your work-life balance?

Follow up: Has it helped?
6 • experience → duration

Have you ever worked in a toxic environment?

How long did it take you to leave?

Follow up: What did you learn?
ONLINE MOVE: Type one answer in the chat. Say a longer version aloud without reading it.

05

Speaking mission — survive the magazine

12 min

You are applying for a demanding role at a fashion magazine. Convince the editor you can handle pressure, defend your experience, and negotiate realistic expectations.

STUDENT • ambitious candidate Describe your career so far, your strengths, and how you deal with stress. Use personal experience as evidence.
TEACHER • intimidating editor Interrupt naturally: “Is that crucial?”, “Have you ever been sacked?”, “Why should I consider you?”
ROUND 1 • 2 MINIntroduce yourself and explain why you want this job.
ROUND 2 • 3 MINUse past experience and recent achievements to persuade.
ROUND 3 • 2 MINNegotiate workload and agree on three realistic rules.
ROUND 4 • 2 MINReverse roles. Challenge the candidate with two tough objections.
ROUND 5 • 1 MINRepair one tense error and reuse the corrected phrase naturally.
ROUND 6 • 1 MINGive a final pitch without notes.
MUST USE: Past Simple + Present Perfect • 10+ vocabulary items from the bank • three follow-up questions • one polite disagreement • one clarification
