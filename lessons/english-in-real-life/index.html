02

Tense workout — meaning in context

Notice → choose → explain → personalise 20 min
A • One week offline — complete the story

Use Present Simple, Present Continuous, Present Perfect or Present Perfect Continuous. Then justify each choice.

Lena usually (take) the subway to work, but this week she (stay) in London. She already (visit) three museums, and she (walk) around the city since 8 a.m. Her hotel windows (overlook) Hyde Park. She normally (edit) photos every evening, but now she (try) a digital detox. She not (check) social media yet, and she (sleep) much better lately. She often (drink) magnesium before bed. At the moment, she (look) for a quiet café. Her tour guide just (send) the address, but Lena (wait) for the full itinerary for twenty minutes.

Meaning check: Which actions are routines? Which are temporary? Which show a result? Which emphasise duration?
Personal link: Change two details so the story becomes true for you.
Retell: Close the text and retell it from memory using “we” instead of “she”.
B • Error clinic — diagnose and repair

Each sentence has one tense error. Correct it and explain how the meaning changes.

  1. I’ve been knowing my tour guide for three days.
  2. She visits London twice so far.
  3. I work from the hotel this week.
  4. How long do you use this travel app?
  5. We already edit all the photos.
  6. Since Monday, I reduce my screen time.
  7. I am usually leaving my phone at home.
  8. They do sightseeing right now.
  9. I have this app since 2023.
  10. One week isn’t being long.
Teacher challenge: Your teacher changes one time marker. Correct the tense immediately.
C • Tense-switch interview

Answer aloud. Then switch perspective without changing the topic. Ask your teacher one follow-up question.

1 • routine → temporary change

What do you usually do on a trip?

What are you doing differently on your next trip?

Follow up: Why are you changing it?
2 • experience → duration

Which European cities have you visited?

How long have you been planning your next journey?

Follow up: What have you decided so far?
3 • fact → current trend

How do phones make travel convenient?

How are travel habits changing now?

Follow up: Is this change positive?
4 • result → recent activity

What have you reduced recently?

What have you been doing to achieve it?

Follow up: What result can you see?
5 • routine → experience

Where do you usually stay when you travel?

What is the best place you have ever stayed in?

Follow up: What made it memorable?
6 • temporary → duration

What are you limiting this week?

How long have you been working on this change?

Follow up: Has it become easier?
ONLINE MOVE: Type one answer in the chat. Say a longer version aloud without reading it.

05

Speaking mission — plan the trip

12 min

Your friend wants a one-week digital-detox trip to London. Give realistic advice, respond to objections and agree on a plan.

STUDENT • experienced traveller Recommend transport, sightseeing and phone rules. Use personal experience as evidence.
TEACHER • sceptical friend Interrupt naturally: “Is that essential?”, “How long have you been doing that?”, “What are you changing now?”
ROUND 1 • 2 MINDescribe your usual travel habits and current plans.
ROUND 2 • 3 MINUse past experience and recent activity to persuade.
ROUND 3 • 2 MINNegotiate three rules and summarise the agreement.
ROUND 4 • 2 MINReverse roles. Challenge the plan with two new objections.
ROUND 5 • 1 MINRepair one tense error and reuse the corrected phrase naturally.
ROUND 6 • 1 MINGive a final recommendation without notes.
MUST USE: all four target tenses • 10+ vocabulary items • three follow-up questions • one polite disagreement • one clarification

TEACHER’S NOTE • ONLINE 1:1 PROCEDURE

Keep the table visible only during input. Hide it for retrieval. Elicit before correcting; use chat for delayed feedback. Prioritise meaning first, then form and pronunciation. During speaking, note errors silently and review only the three that most affect clarity.
